description: |-
  Using the Community Assets and Relative Rurality (CARR) Index and longitudinal records from Kansas and Missouri, this study examines how teacher characteristics and mobility differ across rural settings. Teachers in more rural districts are more likely to leave, and movers tend to shift toward urban areas. The findings highlight the need for retention policies that account for differences among rural communities.
