description: |-
  This study examines whether the benefits of teacher evaluation persist as evaluation systems change. It analyzes revisions to DCPS’s IMPACT system, including tougher standards for lower-performing teachers and less reliance on value-added test scores. Descriptive and causal evidence indicate that teachers facing dismissal were more likely to leave or improve their performance, suggesting that evaluation can continue to support teaching quality over time.
